Metrics

Every metric is computed by a deterministic evaluator. Ratios are normalized to [0, 1] (higher = better); everything else is reported in native units (ms, USD, counts). Full definitions in docs/METRICS.md.

Honest by design

  • Cost is estimated from a transparent char-based token estimator (tokens ≈ chars / k, documented k) times a fixture price table — a figure for relative comparison, never a bill.
  • Structural, not string-equal. Task success checks properties; exact match is opt-in and used sparingly.
  • Placeholders are labeled. Explanation-quality and semantic-similarity (semantic_stub) are deterministic stand-ins, not learned judges.
  • Infra errors are separated and never raise a regression flag.

Aggregation

Suite-level numbers are means / sums / percentiles over per-case MetricResults, computed with the standard-library statistics module only. pandas is an optional convenience for ad-hoc summaries, never required.

Open questions / limitations

Deterministic evidence scoring has a known ceiling versus a learned entailment model; the gap is a documented research direction (see Roadmap).
